Geometry

Both in a 24 × 24 box, what Claude Code implements against. Colour is never hardcoded: Kirigami.Theme.textColor for every stroke and fill.

Dial

  • Track: 270° arc, r 8.5, stroke 2.2, round caps, 28% opacity, gap at the bottom.
  • Level: same arc at 100%, from 135° clockwise, length = (vol − floor) / (ceiling − floor).
  • Hub: r 1.7, full opacity.
  • QML: Shape + PathAngleArc.

Level

  • Ring: full circle, r 8.5, stroke 1.8, 100%.
  • Fill: disc r 6.6 cut by a horizontal chord at height 2·r·fraction from the bottom. 1 px clear gap to the ring.
  • No hub: it would vanish into the fill.
  • QML: Shape with a PathArc segment closed by the chord.
State rule changed from v1–v4: earlier mockups kept showing the volume arc while the amp was off or booting, even though the number had already become a dash. Now both agree: the level and the number only show when the volume is known (normal, muted). Off and booting show the empty outline, dimmed or pulsing. No amp is the same dashed ring for both styles.
Level at the floor is an empty solid ring. It stays distinguishable from off (35% opacity) and no amp (dashed), and the number says the rest.
Muted uses the same treatment for both: level or fill fades to 28%, slash with a panel-coloured halo so it cuts cleanly through the ring, number fades to 40%. The unit is small dB (v4), counted in the number's reserved width; with "Show volume" off the glyph stands alone. The number is hidden automatically on vertical panels regardless of the setting.
Booting is the only animated state, and it respects reduced-motion (steady 60% instead of a pulse).
