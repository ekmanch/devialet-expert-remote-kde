v28: window matched to your live dialog. Colours sampled from the screenshot, sizes measured at its ~1.93× scale. Titlebar, sidebar, page and button bar are all the same #151515, separated only by #414141 hairlines. The page title row ("General") sits above the scroll area, and the OK / Apply / Cancel bar is under the page column only, with the sidebar running to the bottom. Row copy for everything visible in the screenshot is taken from it; rows below the fold (chime, login, defaults) keep the mockup copy, title-cased to match.
Light: the same window in pure #ffffff. Every surface is white, including titlebar, sidebar, button bar and the disabled Apply button; only hairline dividers and card shadows separate things.
New since the live build: the Theme row (Dark / Light / Follow system, default Follow system) at the top of Appearance. It recolours the widget's popup, tooltip and OSD, not this page, which follows the window it lives in.
